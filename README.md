# Product Adoption & Market Expansion Analytics

## Overview

This project demonstrates how customer behavior, product adoption patterns, geographic demand, and customer segmentation can be analyzed to support business growth initiatives.

The objective is to understand how customers interact with subscription-based products, identify conversion opportunities, uncover underserved markets, and generate actionable recommendations for product growth and expansion.

The project combines product analytics, customer analytics, geographic analysis, and business intelligence techniques.

---

## Business Context

Organizations often offer multiple subscription-based services but face challenges in understanding customer adoption behavior and identifying growth opportunities.

Customers may discover products, explore service pages, and demonstrate interest without ultimately completing a purchase. Understanding these behavioral patterns is critical for improving product adoption and supporting business expansion decisions.

This project analyzes customer interactions across the product journey to identify:

* Product adoption opportunities
* High-intent customer segments
* Geographic demand patterns
* Market expansion opportunities
* Customer targeting strategies

---

## Business Objectives

* Analyze customer progression through the product journey.
* Measure product adoption and conversion rates.
* Identify high-intent customers who do not convert.
* Evaluate demand across different customer segments.
* Assess geographic demand and market potential.
* Generate recommendations to support product growth initiatives.

## Key Business Questions

### Product Adoption Analysis

1. How many customers progress through each stage of the journey?
2. Which products receive the highest engagement?
3. Where are the largest conversion drop-offs occurring?

### Customer Opportunity Analysis

4. Which users demonstrate strong interest but do not purchase?
5. Which customer segments have the highest adoption potential?
6. Which segments should be prioritized for targeted outreach?

### Geographic Demand Analysis

7. Which cities generate the highest product demand?
8. Which locations show strong interest but low adoption?
9. Which regions represent expansion opportunities?

### Customer Segmentation Analysis

10. How does adoption vary across customer segments?
11. Which customer groups have the highest conversion rates?
12. Which customer groups show untapped growth potential?
