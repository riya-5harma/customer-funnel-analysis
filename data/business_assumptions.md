# Business Assumptions

## Purpose

This project uses a synthetic dataset designed to simulate customer interactions with subscription-based digital products. The assumptions below are intended to create realistic business scenarios that support product adoption analysis, customer segmentation, funnel analytics, and market expansion decision-making.

---

# Products

The platform offers three subscription-based services:

| Product                | Description                                                     |
| ---------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Fitness Membership     | Access to fitness centers, gym facilities, and workout programs |
| Nutrition Consultation | Personalized nutrition and diet planning services               |
| Wellness Coaching      | Lifestyle, wellness, and preventive health coaching             |

---

# Customer Journey

Customers progress through the following stages:

Homepage Visit

↓

Product Discovery

↓

Product Page Visit

↓

Purchase

Not all customers complete every stage. Funnel drop-offs occur naturally at each step of the journey.

# Funnel Assumptions

The synthetic dataset assumes:

* All users begin with a homepage visit.
* Only a subset of users proceed to product discovery and product page visits.
* Product page visitors have a higher probability of purchase than homepage visitors.
* Conversion rates vary by traffic source, customer segment, and geography.

Expected Funnel Distribution:

| Funnel Stage       | Approximate Users |
| ------------------ | ----------------- |
| Homepage Visit     | 5,000             |
| Product Page Visit | 3,000             |
| Purchase           | 500–700           |

---

# Product Adoption Assumptions

* Fitness Membership is expected to have the highest adoption.
* Nutrition Consultation has moderate adoption.
* Wellness Coaching has comparatively lower adoption.
* High-income customers are more likely to purchase annual plans.
* Medium-income customers are more likely to purchase quarterly plans.
* Low-income customers are more likely to purchase monthly plans.

---

# Geographic Demand Assumptions

Demand is not evenly distributed across locations.

* Tier 1 cities generally show higher adoption and purchase rates.
* Some Tier 2 cities demonstrate strong product interest despite lower service availability.
* Certain cities may exhibit high demand but lower conversion due to limited service coverage.

These scenarios support market expansion and opportunity analysis.

---

# Customer Opportunity Assumptions

Some customers demonstrate strong purchase intent but do not complete a transaction.

Indicators of purchase intent include:

* Multiple product page visits
* Product exploration behavior
* High engagement with product information

These users represent potential candidates for targeted outreach campaigns and conversion optimization initiatives.
